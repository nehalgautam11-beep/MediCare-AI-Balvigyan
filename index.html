Features you can try

Reminders

Smart medicine reminders

Caretakers add each medicine with its dose and times. When a dose is due, MediCare AI makes sure the patient notices (by voice, on screen, with lights and with sound) and records what happened.

  • 1Schedule. Medicines, doses and times are added from the admin panel.
  • 2Alert. Myra speaks, the screen shows the dose, LEDs flash and the buzzer beeps 3 times.
  • 3Record. The answer or a missed dose is saved with the time in A.M./P.M. format.
Alerts: Voice · screen · beepPatients: Up to 3Offline: Yes

Answer a reminder

Taken · Snooze · Skip

When a reminder comes, the patient answers in the easiest way for them, by touching a big button or simply by saying the word. Each answer does something different and is saved in the history.

  • 1Taken. Marks the dose as taken now. Family sees it in the app.
  • 2Snooze. Reminds again after 10 minutes (the caretaker can change this).
  • 3Skip. Records that the dose was skipped, so the doctor knows.

Health monitor

Live ECG

From the Monitor button on the touchscreen, the patient can see their heart's electrical activity as a moving graph, like the ECG screens in hospitals, using the AD8232 sensor and three electrodes.

  • 1Attach electrodes. Three pads on right arm, left arm and right leg (RA, LA, RL).
  • 2Live graph. Arduino #2 sends 250 readings per second; the screen draws the waveform.
  • 3Heart rate. Peaks in the wave are counted to find beats per minute, then saved.
Sampling: 250 per secondSensor: AD8232Saved to: Patient profile

Pulse screen

Heart rate & SpO₂ check

The Pulse screen is a quick, simple health check. The patient places a fingertip on the MAX30100 sensor and holds still for 10 seconds while a countdown runs on the screen.

  • 1Place finger. Tap Monitor → Pulse and rest a fingertip on the sensor.
  • 210-second check. A countdown runs while the reading is taken.
  • 3Result & history. Heart rate and SpO₂ appear in big numbers and are saved for the logged-in patient.
Time: 10 secondsNormal HR: 60–100 bpmNormal SpO₂: 95–100 %

The screen

Elder-friendly touchscreen

The touchscreen was designed for elders: large text, bright colours and only a few big buttons on the home screen. Every screen has a clear Back button, and nothing needs typing.

  • 1Home screen. Clock with A.M./P.M., date, weather, temperature and air quality.
  • 2Big buttons. Ask MYRA, Monitor, Reminders and Setup, each in its own colour.
  • 3Patient PIN. Each patient logs in with a PIN to see their own data.

For every patient

MediCare app

Every patient gets their own login to the MediCare web app. It works on any phone, tablet or laptop and can be added to the home screen like a normal app, with no download from a store needed.

  • 1Log in. Username and password. Forgot it? Contact help@pvproyals.in.
  • 2My medicines. Today's doses, upcoming times and full dose history.
  • 3My health. Saved ECG graphs, heart rate and SpO₂ readings.

For caretakers

Admin panel

The caretaker or family member uses the admin panel to set everything up. It opens from the ADMIN PANEL button at the top-right of the MediCare portal and has its own admin password.

  • 1Patients. Add up to 3 patients, set PINs, languages and app logins.
  • 2Medicines. Add medicines, doses and reminder times for each patient.
  • 3Reports. See dose history, missed doses and health readings.
